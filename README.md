## 🚗 What I'm building

[**MANSORYHUB**](https://github.com/MANSORYHUB) — a UAE-first automotive marketplace where private sellers and dealerships list vehicles, while buyers discover and connect with them directly.

The hard part isn't publishing listings. It's turning discovery into trust and qualified leads:

- **Reviewed listings** — every listing is reviewed before publication
- **Video-first discovery** — short-form videos and Car Stories connect viewers to the exact vehicle through a unique **Car ID**
- **Direct communication** — in-app chat, WhatsApp, or phone, straight to the seller
- **Dealer tools** — inventory management, lead handling, performance insights, paid visibility
- **Trusted partners** — inspection, finance, insurance and warranty through licensed providers

We take **no commission** on vehicle sales. The job is to make discovery faster, listings more trustworthy, and buyer–seller connections more effective.
